# About Saiam Digital Agency

Saiam Digital Agency is a team of strategists, designers, developers and marketers who help ambitious brands grow. We are based in Delhi, India, and serve clients across India, the Middle East, Europe and North America.

## Contact
For new project enquiries, ask this assistant to connect you with an account manager, or use the contact form on our website. Account managers reply within one business day. For urgent issues on an active project, escalate through this assistant and the account manager is alerted immediately.

## Team
Account managers own the client relationship. Specialists (designers, developers, strategists) deliver the work. The Chief of Delivery oversees quality and timelines.
