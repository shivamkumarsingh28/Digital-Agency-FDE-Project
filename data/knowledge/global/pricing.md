# Saiam Digital Agency - Pricing Guide

Final pricing is always confirmed in a written proposal. The ranges below are indicative starting points only.

## Project pricing (starting points)
- Logo and brand identity: from $4,000 to $18,000
- Marketing website: from $8,000 to $30,000
- E-commerce website: from $20,000 to $60,000
- 60-second explainer video: from $3,500 to $12,000
- Custom AI assistant or chatbot: from $6,000 to $25,000

## Monthly retainers
- SEO Starter: $1,500 per month
- SEO Growth: $3,500 per month
- Design and Development Retainer: from $4,000 per month for 40 hours
- Paid media management: 15 percent of ad spend with a $1,200 monthly minimum

## What affects the price
Scope, number of pages or screens, integrations, content volume, timeline and the number of stakeholders involved in review. Rush projects with timelines under half of our standard duration carry a 25 percent surcharge.
