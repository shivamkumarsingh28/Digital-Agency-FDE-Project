# Saiam Digital Agency - Process, Revisions, SLAs and Policies

## Delivery process
Every project follows five stages: Discovery, Strategy, Design and Build, Review, and Launch. You receive a project plan with milestone dates at the end of Discovery. Progress is visible in the portal at all times.

## Revision policy
Each deliverable includes two rounds of revisions unless your proposal says otherwise. A revision round means one consolidated set of feedback. Additional revision rounds are billed at our standard hourly rate. Change requests that alter the agreed scope are handled as a change order and are never started without written approval.

## Review turnaround
Please review deliverables within 5 business days. If we do not hear back within 10 business days, the project timeline may shift and we will notify you of the new dates.

## Support SLA
Retainer clients receive a first response within 4 business hours for urgent issues and within 1 business day for standard requests. Production website outages are treated as priority one and acknowledged within 1 hour during working hours. We cannot guarantee exact resolution times because they depend on the issue.

## Payment terms
Projects are typically billed 50 percent at kickoff and 50 percent on delivery, or in milestone instalments. Retainers are billed monthly in advance. Invoices are due within 15 days of issue. Overdue invoices over 30 days may pause active work. We accept card and bank transfer through the portal.

## Cancellation and refunds
You can cancel a project with 14 days written notice. Work completed up to the cancellation date is billed. Deposits are non-refundable once discovery has started. Retainers can be cancelled with 30 days notice.

## Support escalation
Critical problems can be escalated to your account manager by chat. Escalations are logged and acknowledged during working hours.
