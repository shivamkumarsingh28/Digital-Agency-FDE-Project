# Saiam Digital Agency - Services

Saiam Digital Agency is a full-service digital agency. We design, build and grow brands online. All services are delivered through our white-labeled client portal where you can track progress, review deliverables and pay invoices.

## Brand Identity and Design
We create logos, colour systems, typography, brand guidelines, packaging and social templates. A typical brand identity project takes 4 to 6 weeks and includes three initial logo concepts, two revision rounds and a final brand guidelines PDF. Source files (AI, Figma, SVG, PNG) are delivered on final approval.

## Website Design and Development
We build marketing sites and e-commerce stores using Next.js, React, TypeScript and Tailwind CSS, with headless CMS options. Every site is responsive, WCAG 2.1 AA accessible, and optimised for Core Web Vitals. A marketing website typically takes 6 to 10 weeks; a full e-commerce build takes 10 to 16 weeks. We integrate Stripe, Shopify, HubSpot and most major APIs.

## SEO and Content Marketing
Technical SEO audits, keyword strategy, on-page optimisation, link building and monthly content production. SEO is offered as a monthly retainer with a monthly performance report. Results typically start to show between months 3 and 6.

## Paid Media and Social
Campaign strategy and management across Google Ads, Meta, LinkedIn and TikTok, including creative production, A/B testing and weekly performance dashboards. Minimum recommended ad spend is $2,000 per month; our management fee is separate.

## Video and Motion
Explainer videos, product launch films, social cut-downs and animated logos. A 60-second explainer video typically takes 3 to 5 weeks from script to final delivery.

## AI and Automation
We build custom AI assistants, chatbots, workflow automation and data dashboards for clients, including retrieval-augmented assistants trained on your own documents.

## Retainers
Ongoing support is available as monthly retainers covering design, development, SEO or media, with a guaranteed number of monthly hours and priority scheduling.
