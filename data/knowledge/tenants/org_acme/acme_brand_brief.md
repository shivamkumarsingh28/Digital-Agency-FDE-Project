# Acme Coffee Co. - Confidential Project Brief

## Brand direction
Acme Coffee wants a warm, craft-roasted identity. Primary palette is espresso brown, oat cream and a burnt-orange accent. The tone of voice is friendly and unpretentious. Avoid corporate blue.

## Packaging requirements
Packaging mockups must cover the 250g bag, 1kg bag and the retail gift box. Recyclable kraft stock is required. The legal team must approve the final nutritional and allergen text before print.

## Contract notes
Acme has a signed addendum allowing three revision rounds for the Brand Identity Refresh project instead of the standard two. The website redesign launch date is fixed to the Black Friday campaign and cannot move.
