# Saiam Digital Agency - Frequently Asked Questions

## How do I get started with Saiam?
Share your goals, timeline and budget range through our contact form or with this assistant. An account manager will schedule a discovery call within one business day and send a proposal within three business days after the call.

## How does the client portal work?
Every client gets a branded portal. You can see project status and milestones, review and approve deliverables, leave comments directly on files, download final assets and pay invoices. You can use your own domain (for example portal.yourbrand.com).

## What happens when a deliverable is ready for my review?
The deliverable moves to the status Pending Client Approval and the designated reviewers are notified. You can approve it or request a revision with written feedback. After a revision request the deliverable returns to our team.

## What do the deliverable statuses mean?
Draft means our team is still working on it. In Internal Review means we are doing quality control. Pending Client Approval means it is waiting for you. Approved means it is signed off. Revision Requested means you asked for changes and our team is working on them.

## Who owns the final work?
You own all final approved deliverables. Intellectual property transfers to you once the final invoice is paid. We may display non-confidential work in our portfolio unless you ask us not to.

## What file formats do I receive?
Design work is delivered as Figma, AI, SVG, PNG and PDF. Websites are delivered as a deployed site plus a Git repository. Video is delivered as MP4 and ProRes on request.

## Can you work with our in-house team or existing tools?
Yes. We regularly collaborate with in-house marketers and developers, and we integrate with Slack, Microsoft Teams, Notion, Jira and Google Workspace.

## Do you sign NDAs?
Yes. We are happy to sign your NDA before a discovery call, and all client data in the portal is isolated per organisation and encrypted in transit and at rest.

## How is my data protected?
Each client organisation's data is strictly isolated. We use TLS 1.3 in transit, AES-256 at rest, multi-factor authentication and role-based access control, and we undergo regular security audits.

## What are your working hours and time zone?
Our team works Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00 IST (UTC+5:30), with overlap hours for US and EU clients on request.
